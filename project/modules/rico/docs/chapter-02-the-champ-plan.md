# Rico Chapter 2: The Champ — researched story proposal

Prepared 2026-09-05. This is a proposed expansion, not an implemented script or a claim that new scenes are official canon. It preserves the opening premise and the existing boundary before Chapter 3, The Locked Room.

## Recommended theme

**Rico has learned to survive cruelty by making people fear him. Fei begins to challenge his belief that strength is the only reason someone deserves respect.**

Chapter 1 gives the audience Anne's wish that Rico use his strength to protect people. Chapter 2 confronts them with the adult who administers the Baptism. That contradiction should be the chapter's emotional center. His suffering explains his defenses without excusing the suffering he inflicts.

Keep The Champ as the title. It begins as a position everyone recognizes and ends as a position Rico has lost. The chapter should leave him unsettled and still capable of a destructive decision, with the first basis for trust in Fei. His mature commitment to other people belongs later in the anthology.

The audience can remember Anne's words through contrasting situations. Avoid giving Rico a new, explicit recollection of the childhood promise here: the retail palace-room scene has its own sensory memory trigger. Do not insert a replacement flashback or explain his parentage early.

## What currently exists

The authored [chapter file](../content/chapter_02_the_champ.json) sketches the whole sequence through Rico entering the Central District. The playable implementation currently uses the Champion's sitting room and adjacent bedroom, Hammer's actual stair arrival, a staged Fei conversation, one generic Group League Gear encounter, and conversations/time cuts for most later events.

Hammer currently reports that Fei has awakened in the infirmary. Fei's delivery, the Baptism, sewer investigation, Redrum fight, Finals, and crash are not all implemented cinematic/playable sequences. Expanding them is new work. Preserve the existing room geography and Hammer's reliable arrival behavior while replacing the summaries in deliberate sections.

The chapter-select description currently gives away the murders, Finals, and trap. Proposed replacement premise: a newly arrived Gear pilot draws Rico's attention; the life he has built in D Block is about to be challenged. Let later events emerge during play.

## Canon findings that affect the draft

1. **Rico reportedly refused a pardon.** Hammer reports the refusal and guesses at the reason. A definitive explanation based on protecting prisoners, fear of life outside, or revenge would be new interpretation. The opening should acknowledge his choice without treating any proposed motive as established fact. [Kislev Capital, original English scene transcription](https://xenogears.us/static/media/21_Kislev_Capital.8ee9101583498961b58f.txt)

2. **The sewer resolution is mistaken.** Citan accompanies Rico and Fei. Later dialogue attributes the killings to Id. At this point the characters can accept that the danger is over; an omniscient narrator should not certify Redrum as the murderer. [Sewer Horror](https://xenogears.us/static/media/23_Sewer_Horror.8db5774a9cc831bffc8d.txt), [Shevat's Move, later revelation](https://xenogears.us/static/media/54_Shevats_Move.4bcbc573521dec533745.txt)

3. **The crash's public explanation is incomplete.** Later dialogue describes an assassination attempt disguised as an accident and the Committee's manipulation of Rico. Preserve this distinction; do not establish innocent mechanical failure through private cockpit dialogue. The exact mechanics need not be invented. [Gear Dock Raid](https://xenogears.us/static/media/25_Gear_Dock_Raid.ca3ec30db252d290020a.txt)

4. **The room discovery comes later.** Rico is surprised by the door and its sensory associations. Do not give him advance knowledge of its family connection. [Battling Champ](https://xenogears.us/static/media/24_Battling_Champ.755a54211457f4aa8645.txt)

## Proposed playable chapter

Target approximately 45–60 minutes, subject to scene and battle testing. Keep the point of view with Rico for most of the chapter. Use short transitions to cover Fei's intervening matches and existing retail events that do not need to be replayed in full.

### 1. News from below

Open with a brief view of Fei being delivered to D Block, then move to Rico's rooms. If the delivery is shown, distinguish it from Hammer's later report that Fei is awake.

Let the player explore the Champion's room before Hammer reaches him. A good bed, arena schedule, and a subordinate bringing news show Rico's privileges. A closed route and orders from outside his room establish their limits. Do not repeat the full Chapter 1 backstory.

Hammer arrives with useful information: the prisoner's name, condition, and connection to a captured Gear. His breathlessness comes from urgency. Once he is speaking, let his competence show. Rico wants to know whether the stranger is dangerous; Hammer wants his information to count.

**Player objective:** hear Hammer's report and meet the new inmate.

### 2. The Baptism

Stage the introduction and the initiation so the player actually sees the practice Rico supervises. Keep its violence and the canonical outcome for the anthology's chosen route. Rico is imposing his standard of worth on somebody who has barely recovered.

Fei's resistance to fighting troubles Rico because it makes power and willingness to use it separate things. Do not have Fei diagnose Rico, lecture him about Anne, or win his friendship here. Rico's response remains harsh.

Concentrate the dramatic scene on their confrontation. There is no need to replay all of Fei's preliminary fights at full length in a Rico-led chapter. Any abbreviated version must make the sequence and outcome clear.

**Emotional purpose:** confront the audience with how far the adult Rico's behavior has moved from the childhood they just played.

### 3. The life of the Champ

Add a small D Block circuit: the common area, a preparation space, and return to Rico's rooms. Give a few existing prisoners specific concerns and relationships. Some rely on Rico; some fear him; some benefit from his position. No single conversation should explain the whole prison system.

Keep one short, winnable Stier defense to demonstrate why Rico's reputation is earned. It can adapt the existing Group League encounter, but remains an anthology addition rather than a newly asserted historical match.

Hammer helps with parts, messages, or practical preparations. Rico takes this help for granted. Hammer begins to find a different kind of acknowledgement around Fei. Treat that contrast quietly; Chapter 2 should not advertise Hammer's eventual betrayal or make him secretly malicious already.

Let the refused pardon surface in conversation without resolving it in a speech. Rico can dismiss the question. The player should wonder what leaving would cost him, even when the gate could open.

### 4. People he cannot protect

Introduce the disappearances through changed activity in D Block: an absent familiar face, an unfinished preparation, and a frightened report. The earlier conversations give the losses weight without requiring a separate biography for every victim.

Rico approaches Fei with suspicion, then permits an investigation. Stage his decision to go personally and keep others out of the sewers. These actions expose responsibility beneath his authority, while retaining his anger and the threat in their uneasy alliance.

**Player objective:** investigate with Fei and Citan. The party has a shared immediate problem; friendship is still premature.

### 5. Below D Block

Make this the chapter's principal playable section: a compact sewer route with readable landmarks, a few evidence interactions, and the Redrum encounter. Avoid turning it into a long repetition of the original dungeon.

Rico knows the prison's physical world, Fei helps pursue the immediate problem, and Citan supplies observations they cannot. Give Rico useful judgments and actions so that he leads more than the walking sprite. Let each member contribute without new, early revelations about Solaris or Id.

Preserve the bell and the disturbing suggestion of residual humanity. Rico can be shaken by what they fought without learning Redrum's identity or later history. The chapter should close the characters' investigation provisionally, leaving the original later revelation intact.

Rico's wound provides a transition out of combat. Fei's concern matters because the Finals still make them opponents.

### 6. Before the Finals

Return to the same rooms with changed interactions. The bed is somewhere Rico resists resting; Hammer sees the injury Rico minimizes. Fei's concern is difficult for Rico to interpret because it offers help without demanding submission.

Keep the dialogue short and leave room for pauses. Rico should not give a speech about his mother's lessons. A small concession — accepting a bandage or allowing Hammer to finish tending the arm — can show movement without making him gentle overnight. These are proposed new gestures.

**Original dialogue sketch, not a retail quotation:**

> **Hammer:** Hold still. I can't tie it if you keep pulling away.
>
> **Rico:** It's tight enough.
>
> **Hammer:** It was tight enough before you put your fist through the practice bag.
>
> Rico stops moving. Hammer finishes the knot.

### 7. The title passes

Preserve Fei's victory. Stage the decisive exchanges and the aftermath rather than making the player complete five repetitive rounds ending in a concealed forced loss. A full controllable Rico-side tournament would be a separate mechanics task; it is not necessary for the emotional beat.

Rico refuses to reduce Fei's victory to an excuse about the injury. Then revisit familiar spaces as privilege changes hands. A clerk directs someone else to the room; people change how they address him. Keep the institutional transition understated.

The important new scene is Rico having to receive respect from Fei after losing the status that he thought produced respect. He need not know what to do with it yet.

### 8. The dangerous decision

Retain the arena incident and flight toward the Central District, respecting the later account of the assassination attempt. Losing the title does not instantly free Rico from his anger. His next decision can remain destructive even after the beginnings of trust with Fei.

Avoid a definitive private malfunction monologue, an invented fully explained sabotage device, or a premature reveal of every Committee plan. A brief, carefully limited incident sequence and its public aftermath are sufficient.

End with Rico entering the Central District and seeking concealment. Do not have him announce that he is going to his mother's room or that he already knows his father. Chapter 3 owns the door, the recognition, and the family confrontation in the anthology.

## Writing priorities

- **Rico:** terse, observant, proud, and capable of uncomfortable silence. His harshness must have consequences. Sympathy comes from behavior, contradictions, and loss; it does not require excusing the Baptism.
- **Hammer:** practical knowledge, fast thinking, humor, and a need to be useful. Limit repeated stuttering. Show a relationship that can later be mourned, without explaining his entire future arc now.
- **Fei:** a rival with his own aversion to violence and immediate need to escape. He challenges Rico through behavior, not a therapy speech.
- **Citan:** present in the sewer party; measured about the evidence and limited in what he tells the others. Preserve his knowledge boundary and later reveal.
- **Anne:** her existing Chapter 1 scenes remain the audience's context. Use parallels in actions, not repeated flashbacks or new recovered memories.

## Suggested implementation order

1. Finish the opening through the Baptism as a coherent 10–15 minute slice. Preserve Hammer's entrance and bedroom access; add only the locations and actors this slice needs.
2. Build the D Block circuit and compact sewer section, including a real Redrum encounter and the three-character party.
3. Add the injury aftermath, dedicated Finals presentation, title transfer, and Central District transition.
4. Remove obsolete narrative summaries, revise chapter-select exposition, and run a full continuity/dialogue pass.
5. Validate objective order, repeated interactions, save/reload, side-room return, party changes, and chapter completion in standalone and combined profiles. Do not reuse Mass Driver's claimed fields when adding Rico locations.

Before implementation, correct the draft's narrator-level Redrum certainty and private Stier-malfunction claims. These edits are part of the proposed writing pass; this planning document does not change the playable chapter.
