# Mass Driver — revised dialogue

29 September 2026 — terminal content overhaul following the second dialogue pass. **The approved prose is installed in the authored sources for V171 (`mass-driver-v171-dialogue`).** This document preserves the editorial reading layout; the [V171 transcript](../mass-driver-dialogue-v171.md) records the actual compiled page divisions. Build evidence belongs in [V171 candidate notes](../mass-driver-v171.md).

Read the scenes below as conversations and documents, not as finished text-box pages. Optional records remain optional; conditional messages are labeled. Routine item and menu text is included for completeness. Dormant dialogue is excluded.

The opening establishes a Gear-sized exterior entrance connecting directly to the hangar. Elly and Emeralda take the personnel route through the dungeon; Citan brings their Gears in from outside before joining them at Central Control. V171 implements this as an off-screen arrangement established in the opening and acknowledged at the hangar. It does not add a traversable exterior route or delivery animation.

The original Miang memory, its entry and return, and all its staging remain unchanged. The existing scene plays at the marked point in Central Control.

The terminal overhaul assigns each computer a purpose supported by its location: train service, staff attendance, power distribution, engineering tests, inventories, access records, defense control, or magazine handling. Historical files are identified as stored records; operating screens describe only functions the current scripts perform. Routine staff detail and the occasional personal note remain, but terminals no longer all deliver successive chapters of correspondence. The Miang scene and the hopeful launch remain intact.

**Elly:** “We have to send Taura's capsule into the upper atmosphere. Once the nanomachines spread, they should remove the Limiter.”

**Emeralda:** “I'm ready, Elly.”

**Elly:** “All right. Let's go.”

*Citan brings both Gears into the hangar directly from outside, then goes on foot to Central Control. Neither Gear passes through the dungeon corridors or personnel lift. A delivery cutaway remains to be designed: it must account for how Citan moves two parked Gears, without assuming remote control or that he can pilot Crescens. The existing sealed north transfer door is not yet an exterior entrance; using it would require checking its geometry and changing its door state and error-only interaction. The eventual Gear departure must also agree with the exterior route.*

## Editorial basis and remaining integration

This pass reread the original English departure, Solaris, Ethos, Anima, and Zeboim passages below. Their useful patterns are specific: Elly can argue, qualify what she knows, and act under pressure; Emeralda already chose to accompany her and need not win permission again; technical documents and private exchanges have different voices. These links lead to unofficial transcriptions of game text; the new Mass Driver writing is original mod material.
