# Xenogears research: Disc 1 close reading and character evidence

Research date: 2026-09-16. Scope: English front matter and all 55 Disc 1 chapter files served by xenogears.us. This is pass 1 and the Disc 1 contribution to pass 3 of the requested five-pass research. No game assets, dialogue, code, configuration, builds or saves were changed. This document is research, not a content revision.

The strongest lesson for Mass Driver is that faithful writing requires **the right person to know, notice, conceal and care about the right thing at this exact point in the story**. Copying surface habits—Citan explaining, Elly comforting, Emeralda speaking in fragments—would miss what these scenes do. All three have wider registers than those shorthand descriptions imply.

All retained blocks were read in chapter order, including tutorials, item text, NPCs, alternate party members, choices and later-state variants. No emulator or event-script reachability audit was performed. A present line cannot become a mandatory scene or established knowledge acquisition without checking its condition. [D1-10] [D1-41]

This pass reads the **English localization in an annotated extraction/transcript corpus**. ## Character evidence for the Mass Driver moment

### Elly: conscience, competence and unresolved pain

**Observed in the English scenes:** Compassion develops through criticism of her own behavior. In the forest she attacks Fei's avoidance while struggling with it herself. During the purge briefing she identifies excessive firepower and the operational absurdity of withholding objectives, then insists the prospective victims are human. At Nortune she initially retreats into duty, then risks herself to reduce damage. Her moral authority develops through responsibility, not freedom from prejudice or anger. [D1-07] [D1-23] [D1-26]

The raft conversation is a particularly useful reference. She answers Fei's fear that his altruism is selfish by discussing the emergency ration she shared. This extended philosophical exchange grows from an action both have experienced. She entertains partial, imperfect motives rather than requiring moral completeness. Its final unfamiliar memory is a flicker, not a solved reincarnation lecture. [D1-29]

She is an investigator and officer as well as an emotional lead. At the Ethos data bank she translates difficult encoded text, resists Bart's impatience and explains its political meaning. In Solaris she switches convincingly to rank and procedure; she knows some routes only vaguely and speculates about others. In Krelian's facility she notices armor construction, then assembles evidence that Citan's access and knowledge cannot have the innocent explanation Fei wants. [D1-37, field 1324][D1-37] [D1-48, field 1654][D1-48] [D1-49, field 1662][D1-49] [D1-51, field 1750][D1-51] [D1-52, field 1738][D1-52]

Her register changes with relationship. She scolds Fei, becomes embarrassed, teases him in her bedroom, fights bitterly with her father, apologizes to her mother, and answers Emeralda's possessiveness with a sharp joke. A solemn ruin does not require every line to be serene or elevated. At the end of Disc 1, however, her parents' deaths and commitment to Fei are recent experiences, not distant biographical facts. [D1-47] [D1-50] [D1-53] [D1-55]

**Interpretation for Mass Driver:** Let her notice people affected by a mechanism, then decide with military and technical understanding. Let her redirect Citan or challenge an assumption. When a record touches parenthood, abandonment or coercion, favor specificity and hesitation over a generic speech about humanity. An unfinished personal sentence need not make her passive. Her voice need not always resemble either a saint or the defensive lieutenant in the forest.

**Memory boundary:** Zeboim already produces involuntary recognition, first-person bodily recollection, a ritual utterance and defense of the child. Elly then asks what she is doing and who she is. At recruitment she still asks who Kim is and who created Emeralda. Familiarity is canonical; complete conscious recovery of the family biography is not established here. Avoid either total unfamiliarity or effortless omniscient recall. [D1-39, fields 1372/1344/1364/1362][D1-39] [D1-47, field 1170][D1-47]

### Citan: a curious, humane and morally difficult guide

**Observed:** Citan tends toward complete sentences, formal courtesy, qualified inference and sequential explanation. This is a tendency, not a ban on contractions, exclamations, jokes or terse orders. At Dazil he links a mechanical problem to supply conditions; at the Ethos he distinguishes a pattern in records from confirmation; at Babel he proposes a model, admits what he cannot explain and takes responsibility for danger. [D1-10] [D1-37] [D1-46]

His curiosity has comic excess. During the Gear theft he explains a repair part's advantages and fuel cost while escape is urgent. Fei interrupts; Citan acknowledges the habit and returns to the task. The game dramatizes the tension between interesting explanation and immediate need. Use that tension sparingly instead of deleting every digression or repeating one at every terminal. [D1-25, field 1116][D1-25]

Care is practical. He repairs equipment, arranges treatment, delegates rescue, wants the sewer victim buried, and attends Margie's wound before pursuit. He is also calculating: he presses Elly to leave Fei early, threatens to shoot her to manage Bart's distrust after the hypnosis, and deliberately exposes Fei and Elly to Soylent's horror. His later explanation admits deception while defending the need to see reality. Neither secret malice nor uncomplicated avuncular kindness describes all this. [D1-08] [D1-23] [D1-32] [D1-44] [D1-51] [D1-52]

His competence has limits. He underestimates danger on the sea route before Bart shoots the Goliath down. He reaches an entry without a complete access plan. His account of Fei distinguishes observations, inferences and unanswered questions. The translated psychological terms belong to the fictional scene, not independent clinical authority. Furthermore, an informed character can withhold information: uncertainty and concealment cannot always be distinguished through phrasing alone. [D1-28] [D1-50] [D1-54]

**Interpretation for Mass Driver:** Technical lines work when they identify evidence, explain a consequence, or change an action. He should sometimes seek another person's observation or acknowledge a limit. He can delight in surviving machinery and be amused by a companion; making him solely a grave encyclopedia is less faithful. He has now chosen Fei's group and helped their escape, without that commitment erasing his prior manipulation. [D1-53] [D1-55]

**Launch reference:** Babel moves from substantial collaborative explanation to short assignments, readiness checks, corrections and confirmation. Distinguish briefing from execution. This is a particularly close dramatic and technical analogue for Mass Driver. [D1-46, fields 0908/1440][D1-46]

### Emeralda: memory, attachment and agency before growth

**Observed:** Recruitment disproves a blanket rule that child Emeralda must speak only broken English. In one scene she repeats Kim's name, protests adult categories, gives a sustained, largely grammatical dream account, describes a birthday-like image without its ordinary name, recalls disappearance and darkness, seeks a promise and becomes possessive toward Elly. Citan tentatively interprets Kim relationally; Fei accepts responsibility without fully understanding. [D1-47, field 1170][D1-47]

The distinction is less grammatical ability than **what matters to her and how she identifies it**. She knows a person and attachment more confidently than the adults' vocabulary for her creation. Her unfamiliar-object description is concrete and perceptual; her interruption is emotionally motivated. She should not exist exclusively to ask the question that lets Citan explain something to the player.

During the Solaris escape she recognizes Fei, wants to accompany him and excitedly repeats that they have found an exit. When Fei leaves at the end of Disc 1 she speaks very briefly and in fragments. Both are pre-growth examples: variation with situation, not a linear grammar progression. [D1-53, fields 1798/1638][D1-53] [D1-55, field 1198][D1-55]

Shevat return variants associate her perceptions with damaged humans and ending life. These inform a possible sensory vocabulary but cannot date that knowledge to the first Shevat visit. Early-dungeon variants marked possibly cut are weaker evidence for a playable scene. [D1-41, field 1474][D1-41] [D1-44]

**Interpretation for Mass Driver:** Preserve remembered attachment, distress at disappearance, direct wants and the ability to notice what adults miss. Mix repetition and fragments with clear statements when justified. Test abstract conclusions against what she has perceived or remembered, rather than rejecting grammatical sentences. Being a nanomachine colony does not by itself grant access to every old terminal, protocol or historical fact.

**Growth boundary:** This pass ends before the optional lighthouse. Disc 1 already supplies Kim memories and fluent speech, but not a demonstrated, integrated account of her identity and acceptance of her past. Establish the lighthouse's precise additions from that later scene rather than her appearance or grammar.

### Miang: persuasive care and private control

**Observed:** In Aveh she can be an efficient aide and offer conditional protection. On the Gebler ship she defends Elly procedurally, removes her from Dominia's abuse, offers soothing care, and subsequently reports preparations to disable the enemy ship. The hypnosis makes the care-shaped language retrospectively disturbing. At Babel she gives concise operational orders; privately with Grahf or Krelian she speaks far more knowingly about motives and outcomes. [D1-16] [D1-31, fields 1254/1256/1150][D1-31] [D1-32] [D1-40] [D1-53]

**Interpretation:** Her menace often works through existing desires, relationships and institutions. Ask which persona she needs before each listener; do not require every appearance to advertise the same cosmic identity. Private player-facing information does not automatically enter party knowledge. This observation does not authorize changes to Mass Driver's protected Miang flashback, boundaries or staging.

## Twelve scene-grounded writing rules

1. **Give technical explanation a present reason.** Dazil's parts change the route; Ethos records change Billy's trust; Babel's model determines assignments. A Mass Driver explanation should enable a choice, expose a consequential obstacle or reframe evidence. Not every mechanical noun requires a paragraph. [D1-10] [D1-37] [D1-46]

2. **Allow length when an exchange develops.** The raft reflection is substantial. Its strength is the shared ration and Fei's objection, not brevity. An arbitrary word limit cannot determine whether dialogue changes a relationship. [D1-29]

3. **Separate knowledge, inference and intrusive memory.** Elly's Zeboim sensations arrive without an explanatory model. Let her uncertainty remain distinct from Citan's technical reading. [D1-39]

4. **Make Elly an operator and decision-maker.** Her purge objections are tactically literate; she reads records and challenges Citan. In a launch facility she can understand, decide, translate or direct, rather than merely confirm his conclusions. [D1-23] [D1-37] [D1-52]

5. **Use Emeralda's priorities, not uniform grammar.** Recruitment mixes coherent memory with repetition and jealousy. Sensory observation, demand or refusal can be more characteristic than a generic naïve question. [D1-47] [D1-53] [D1-55]

6. **Let Citan's curiosity have a human edge.** Surviving machinery can interest him; a companion can hurry him. His repair-item self-correction is a model, but repeating it everywhere turns a trait into a routine. [D1-25]

7. **Change guidance with player state.** The cavern distinguishes remaining interlocks; Shevat offers a brief repeat summary. Mass Driver should identify remaining actions without repeating already understood history. [D1-12] [D1-43]

8. **Let physical evidence carry emotion.** Shevat's child room, the orphan's bell and restored music work through specific things. Existing workers' records and vault objects can do the same without a moral explanation after every discovery. [D1-04] [D1-35] [D1-41]

9. **Distinguish ordinary voices.** Workers, citizens and salvagers discuss wages, repair, family, status, fear and pride. Existing archived staff should have work and relationships as well as exposition. This is not a demand for more rooms or NPCs. [D1-10] [D1-24] [D1-30] [D1-38]

10. **Let urgency change syntax and subject.** Babel's orders differ from its planning; Nortune's action differs from the raft reflection. Optional background should wait for an appropriate interaction once an irreversible launch or immediate threat begins. [D1-26] [D1-29] [D1-46]

11. **Respect loss without eliminating humor.** Warmth, embarrassment and comedy often adjoin pain. Ask who jokes, why, and whether the scene recognizes its cost—not whether every room maintains one emotional temperature. [D1-29] [D1-35] [D1-39] [D1-47]

12. **Do not turn source ambiguity into invented certainty.** Distinguish doctrine, documents, rumor, antagonist scenes and interpretation. Before a line declares historical fact, establish what its speaker encountered. Missing text and unused variants cannot establish that. [D1-15] [D1-37] [D1-41] [D1-44] [D1-52]

## Corrections to overly broad initial judgments

- “Fluent child Emeralda is wrong” is unsupported. Recruitment disproves it. Inspect the scope and integration of knowledge, not sentence length. [D1-47]
- “Elly should always be gentle” is unsupported. She is compassionate, sharp, technically capable and sometimes defensive; relationship and development set the register. [D1-07] [D1-23] [D1-50] [D1-52]
- “Citan should only give concise facts” is unsupported. Technical enthusiasm and philosophy are canonical; companions and circumstances can interrupt or contest them. [D1-04] [D1-25] [D1-46] [D1-54]
- “All exposition is un-Xenogears” is unsupported. Long explanations, optional institutional history, tutorials and technical screens are part of the original. Placement, function and knowledge matter more than mere existence. [D1-22] [D1-37] [D1-41]
- An elevated Mass Driver phrase should be checked against Disc 2 narration before condemnation. The previous audit identified the vault-five tombstone judgment as an adaptation of that narration. Fidelity as homage and fit as live conversation are separate questions for the Disc 2 pass; this document does not independently adjudicate that passage.

## Limits of the result

This does not recover an unpublished official Mass Driver layout, its room-by-room dialogue or a lost authorial plan. It does not certify runtime, cameras, condition reachability or how many mod pages play on a single route. English-only study cannot establish exact Japanese diction. It provides a traceable English narrative baseline and distinguishes canonical fact, plausible extension and editorial taste.

## Adjacent Disc 2 boundary check

After the assigned pass, chapter 56, [Shot Down](https://xenogears.us/static/media/56_Shot_Down.b7677cf681c300305ed8.txt), was also read in full as context. Its coverage belongs to the separate Disc 2 report. Chapter 55's Shevat escape and chapter 56's Taura departure are different events, separated by the shootdown and three weeks of treatment. Citan helping the first escape must not be substituted for his later promise to accompany Elly and join her after finishing his work. Emeralda's interest in following Fei at Shevat likewise does not erase her later insistence on going with Elly.

Chapter 56 repeats the Sophia portrait scene through Fei's and Elly's dream perspectives; this is narrative framing, not evidence that identical historical conversations occurred twice. Both narrators describe their dreams as largely inaccessible after waking. Elly's resulting change of purpose is real without implying detailed conscious recovery of every prior life. At Taura's, she explicitly wants to consider her feelings apart from Fei; Citan's maternal comparison and her protest at being only eighteen preserve humor amid that resolve.
