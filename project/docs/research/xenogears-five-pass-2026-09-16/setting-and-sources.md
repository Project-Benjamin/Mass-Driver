# Pass 2: setting book, translation audit, and creator sources

Research date: 2026-09-16. This pass changes only this research document. It does not change dialogue, scripts, assets, configuration, build selection, or the protected Miang sequence.

The most consequential result is a correction to commonly circulated English chronology: the Japanese setting book supports Kim refusing Ravine in 6082 and discovering Miang's purpose in 6083. An older translation reverses or changes these points. We can now distinguish a translation error from a deliberate mod invention.

This is a substantial targeted reading, not a claim to have mastered every page, every optional interaction, or every Japanese line in Xenogears. The reading ledger below records the actual limits.

## Broad lore: distinctions the writing audit must preserve

The relevant book reading separates Deus, its system, Zohar, Kadomony, and the Wave; separates reincarnation from Miang awakening; and separates the Limiter from humanity's origin and Zeboim's genetic crisis. Cain, Gazel, Miang, and Krelian have distinguishable motives. See PW-V 10-11, 21-29, 68-77, 158-175.

My audit rule is therefore to reject a single explanatory shortcut that makes every illness, memory, power, technological system, and villain intention interchangeable. When dialogue uses “nanomachines,” identify which application the speaker is discussing. When it uses “God,” identify the speaker and their understanding. When it invokes an ancient memory, identify whose experience is available, how clearly, and at which story stage.

The book contains passages labeled as consideration/theory, interpretive commentary, developer remarks, and translator commentary. These are not all equally firm statements of fictional fact. The afterword itself preserves unresolved questions (PW-V 302-303). An adaptation should not accidentally settle those questions through a confident archive terminal.

Apparent discrepancies encountered in the reader include twin numbering (12 versus 166/170), collapse percentages (16 versus 160), Zephyr's birth date/age (14 versus 193), and incompatible age arithmetic in Citan's profile (178-180). These were **not all checked against Japanese**, so they remain translation/transcription/book inconsistency candidates. Do not silently invent reconciliations or treat these as established errors in Square's original print text. None needs to be imported into Mass Driver.

## Consequences for Mass Driver

1. **Separate calendar repair from rewriting history.** The continuous news material presently opens with 6081 in [the defense archive](../../../modules/mass_driver/content/field_394_defense_archive.authored.json), line 250. Its general nuclear-war ending should be checked against the 6083 anchor. If several recordings are intended, make their separate dates perceptible. This is a concrete continuity issue, unlike a merely compatible 6078/6079 administrative visit.

2. **Distinguish the player's inference from a character's conclusion.** Book knowledge is available to the author reviewing a scene; it is not automatically available to Elly, Emeralda, or a Zeboim employee. For every archive revelation, check whether it provides evidence, prompts a question, or establishes an answer. Those effects have different costs for subsequent scenes.

3. **Preserve the lighthouse's dramatic work.** PW-V 194-195 and 203 place Emeralda's fuller memory/identity development there. My inference is that an early optional archive can echo family, recognition, and uncertainty without completing her later understanding. Compare the exact early reaction with the later released dialogue before deciding that any individual line crosses this boundary.

4. **Treat the protected Miang vision as authored connective material.** PW-V 166-171 provides the relationship framework; it does not establish this precise early Disc 2 vision. Compatibility is a question of presentation and knowledge, not a recovered-scene claim. The vision, its boundaries, and staging remain untouched under [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md), line 35. This pass does not recommend routine changes to that protected material.

5. **Avoid making every old document omniscient.** My editorial recommendation is that records have a plausible author, access level, immediate purpose, and incomplete viewpoint. The Mass Driver's employees should sound like people doing work under pressure; their losses need not also explain the game's metaphysics. An unnamed adviser or unexplained authorization may preserve more narrative value than a terminal explaining the whole plan.

6. **Keep the campaign's operation and its later consequences distinct.** Compare the actual release script before attributing timing, intent, or causality to Krelian in a new bridge scene. The other research pass owns that exact sequence. The launcher mission should not gain an invented explanatory conclusion simply because the author knows what happens next.

7. **Keep a provenance column during the final content review.** Useful labels are released scene, official-book detail, translation choice, mod invention, and unresolved. A line may be well written and compatible while remaining an invention. This is a strength of a spiritual reconstruction when documented honestly.

## Creator evidence and its limits

Takahashi describes a schedule/staffing compromise to finish the story, rather than confirming the familiar budget-only explanation. [DEV-2017](https://kotaku.com/the-real-story-behind-xenogears-unfinished-disc-2-1796151112)

The setting book's director comment acknowledges corrections relative to the game (PW-V 288). That makes source disagreements something to examine, not automatically dismiss. The operational choice for this mod should be explicit: where an official-book addition creates a different impression from the released scene, which experience is the expansion meant to preserve?

Tanaka's later interview distinguishes his initial conception of a restrained Emeralda from the more expressive character he saw in the finished game. Mitsuda discusses religious, ethnic, and orchestral musical themes, and collaborative choices about scene placement. Those are translated first-person recollections, not recovered Mass Driver direction. [DEV-2018](https://xenomira.wordpress.com/2018/05/30/xenogears-20th-anniversary-concert-pamphlet-translation/)

My inference: matching the finished game's performances matters more than mechanically imposing an early concept description. A convincing reconstruction should also leave room for collaborators' final choices, humor, awkwardness, and character-specific reactions; “more solemn” is not automatically “more authentic.”

No examined source supplies the mod's complete dungeon script, its puzzle progression, staff-letter story, Guardian encounter, or protected vision. This research supports an informed adaptation, not a claim that those scenes were recovered from Square.

## Resolved and still open

Resolved to a useful standard: book versus patch identity; page-level reading scope; the two substantial old-translation errors in Kim's chronology; the 6083 war anchor; official support for the civilian-to-military launcher premise.

Still requiring scene-level judgment: how much ancient history optional Mass Driver records can reveal without weakening later discoveries; whether particular employee records plausibly know their claims; the precise player's interpretation of the protected vision. These are adaptation decisions, not unanswered factual searches that require indefinite research.

Still requiring original-language checking if used: the nonessential profile/date discrepancies listed above, exact naming conventions across localization editions, and details from unexamined book pages. A later need for one of those details should trigger a targeted source check rather than a claim that this pass already verified it.
