# Canon boundary: Mass-Driver sequence

## Confirmed by the retail Disc 2 presentation

- The mission occurs after Fei departs Taura's home for Ignas and confronts
  Ramsus in Vendetta.
- Elly and Emeralda travel on foot to a functioning ruin of the Zeboim
  civilization called the Mass-Driver facility.
- The interior contains missiles and giant rockets left from the Zeboim era.
- Citan reaches the facility separately and assists before Elly activates the
  Mass Driver.
- The party launches a capsule carrying Taura's nanomachines.
- The nanomachines spread through the upper atmosphere and remove humanity's
  Limiter seal.
- The retail story continues immediately into the wider consequences of the
  Limiter's removal and Krelian's countermeasure.

## Unspecified and therefore ours to design

- Exact floor plan, room count, route, puzzles, security systems, and enemies.
- How Citan entered the facility or what he did before catching up with Elly.
- Whether the facility is defended intentionally or merely hazardous with age.
- The detailed launch procedure and any complications during it.

The playable version should not introduce a major villain or revelation that
would contradict the retail story's description that the party reached the
control room with relatively little difficulty.
