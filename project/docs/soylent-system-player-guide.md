# Soylent System 1.1 — player guide

Launch `PLAY_SOYLENT_SYSTEM.cmd` from the project root, then choose Soylent. Confirm is **K** in your current keyboard setup. Doors and terminals require a fresh confirm press; walking against a door does not open it. Face a computer's front before interacting.

This release keeps one-hit kills enabled at your request. A damaging hit defeats each enemy; a miss or non-damaging action does not. The Quarantine keycard room contains no patient or doctor.

## Route and objectives

1. **Intake Reception and Classification Archive.** Register a cover identity at the Archive. Return to reception and wait for that alias to be called, then follow the physician to the clinic.
2. **Clinic.** Refuse the bloodwork twice when prompted. The confrontation leads to the security battle and the Security keycard. The physician leaves; you can return to Intake.
3. **Examination rooms.** Use the terminal in the unoccupied authorization room and choose to take the **Quarantine keycard**. It is separate from the Security keycard. Other occupied rooms offer optional patient records and staff notes.
4. **Quarantine Diagnostics.** Select biological readings at the four pods, then submit at the east door. Each interaction toggles that reading; the message states its resulting status. Unsubmitted readings reset if you leave. An accepted diagnosis stays complete.
5. **Service Manifold and its annexes.** Visit the Organic Resonance Annex for the five-tone pattern, then enter the directions at the Service Manifold's input panel. Use the scanner in the Clinical Interface Scanner Annex to arm the living-subject cutoff. The decommissioned deep-service shaft is permanently closed and is not part of the route; its panel includes a **Service directory** describing the available destinations.
6. **Stabilization Ward.** The production control offers entry, HP/EP recovery, saving, a **Clearance report**, and **Next objective**. The report identifies missing authorizations; Next objective gives one task based on current progress. Reading clues alone does not grant their corresponding clearance.
7. **Molecular Sorting and M-Project Core.** Controls show their current setting immediately. Choose **Review instructions** for the full explanation, then configure all three controls in each room and use its forward exit to commit. Choices are reversible. Uncommitted settings reset on leaving; completed rooms stay solved. On returning to completed Sorting, the biological material remains stationary.
8. **Meld Containment.** Inspect the containment display and finish the conversation before proceeding.
9. **Generation Chamber.** Save near the entrance. Confirm the Sufal confrontation, win the battle, and finish the epilogue. The **SOYLENT SYSTEM COMPLETE** message closes the dungeon's story. Save your progress, then use the emergency return to revisit the wards and survivors. One of the occupied treatment-room physicians has an optional care-planning conversation with Citan. The local line stays shut down.

## Puzzle solutions

| Puzzle | Solution |
|---|---|
| Quarantine | **Include** Genome, Limiter and Nanomachines. **Exclude** Caste Index. Use the east door to submit. |
| Organic Resonance | **Up, Down, Up, Left, Right.** Enter this at the Service Manifold input panel after hearing the annex clue. |
| Sorting feed A — living tissue | **Stasis assay** |
| Sorting feed B — nanomachine medium | **Reclamation drain** |
| Sorting feed C — unstable Meld | **Containment return** |
| Core — Fusion / Pattern / Containment | **OFF / OFF / ON** |

The Sorting and Core clues also appear in the optional staff memoranda. Reading them grants no progression and is never required.

The original memoranda remain readable after shutdown. **Current status** at the occupied clinic terminals is a separate option, reporting whether transfers remain active or have halted.

## Saving and updates

Use the native save menu at the Stabilization Ward or Generation Chamber. Recovery at the Ward restores character HP and EP. The completion state is awarded after the entire epilogue closes; the save control remains usable afterward.

Restart the launcher when switching builds. An emulator savestate can retain a room's old loaded scripts until you leave and enter it again. A savestate made during battle can retain old enemy HP. This release does not edit your memory cards or savestates.
