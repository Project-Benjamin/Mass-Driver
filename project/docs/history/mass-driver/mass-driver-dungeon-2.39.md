# Mass Driver Dungeon 2.39 - v62 Canon and Story Closure

Version 62 closes the standalone Mass Driver dungeon as one continuous story.
The rewrite removes the former freight-system interpretation and follows the
facility's actual dramatic arc: a machine created to send materials and
instruments toward space was converted into a military missile launcher,
helped destroy the Zeboim world, and is finally used by Elly to carry Taura's
nanomachines into the upper atmosphere.

This remains a canon-conscious reconstruction of a journey described but not
made playable in the released game. It is not recovered Square content.

## Canon sources and limits

The audit used two primary local sources:

- the retail Disc 2 script, especially `57_Break_the_Seal.txt` and its matching
  passage in `FULL_SCRIPT.txt`;
- the English Perfect Works material in `Perfect Works.html`, especially
  printed pages 12-13 (PDF pages 19-20).

Those sources establish the following:

- Elly and Emeralda travel to the still-functioning Zeboim Mass Driver
  facility, where Citan meets them in Central Control.
- The ruin contains missiles and giant rockets which Elly compares to
  tombstones in a graveyard.
- The Mass Driver uses magnetic attraction and repulsion. It was originally
  built to send materials from the surface toward outer space, then adapted
  into a military launcher for missiles.
- Miang served as adviser to a Zeboim head of state, manipulated events from
  behind the political leadership, and helped drive worsening international
  relations toward nuclear war.
- Lawine's destruction of the central power station left half the capital
  without power during the final crisis.
- Zeboim's shortened lives, rising infertility, Kim's molecular-machine
  research, its official rejection, Miang's sponsorship, and the later
  military seizure of Kim's facility belong to the same historical period.
- In the present, Taura's nanomachine capsule is launched through the Mass
  Driver. Its dispersal removes humanity's Limiter and returns the story to
  the retail Disc 2 sequence.

Perfect Works describes materials being sent toward space; it does not
specifically identify a named exploration-probe program. The v62 language
about instrument packages, unmanned carriers, surveys, and a probe seeking
another world is a user-directed, canon-compatible interpolation. It gives the
original space-development purpose a human face without replacing a stated
canon use.

The following details are reconstructed mechanics or original v62 writing,
not retail facts:

- the exact dungeon plan, switches, archive annexes, reserve-magazine route,
  Guardian, Defense Archive, and five-stage authorization puzzle;
- Directive 0320 and the authority points `A-2`, `B-5`, `C-8`, and `D-4`;
- the employee names Lena, Aron, Mara, and Mira, their personal mail, and the
  particular orders attributed to facility staff;
- the number and distribution of surviving reserve magazines, the Battery One
  label, and the surviving local launch logs;
- Miang's apparent presence in the Central Control afterimage.

The reconstruction does not claim that ordinary employees knew Miang's true
purpose, that every surviving rocket still carries a usable nuclear warhead,
or that the afterimage is an objective recording.

## The employee-mail chronology

The active terminals now form a chronological correspondence instead of a set
of disconnected exposition panels.

1. **6072 - The sky as possibility.** The transit archive describes an
   instrument package, materials for space, and an unmanned carrier receiving
   its first velocity from the accelerator. Lena tells Aron that Mira smiled
   when she heard the probe might find another world.
2. **6074 - An ordinary night launch.** Lena remembers staff cheering as a
   carrier cleared the clouds while Mira slept holding a paper star. Border
   alerts and worsening clinic reports are already part of daily life, but the
   launch still represents hope.
3. **6078 - Political access becomes military access.** Aron reports that the
   head of state's new aide brought Strategic Command into the facility and
   asked how quickly an instrument carrier could become a missile. His
   technical answer becomes the opening the conversion needed.
4. **6079 - Objection without power.** Mara refuses to approve strategic
   warheads under the civil launch charter. An executive liaison preserves her
   objection while using emergency law to make her signature unnecessary.
   Another handling mail observes that the launcher itself has not changed;
   the purpose imposed on it has.
5. **6081 - Fear becomes policy.** After Lawine destroys the central power
   station, command demands that the magnetic banks be bypassed and readied.
   The aide's office repeats that readiness is peace and hesitation invites
   attack. Aron wants to believe deterrence will protect his family; Lena sees
   every nation's claimed shield becoming another nation's first threat.
6. **6081 - A real crisis is made useful.** Medical mail records a life
   expectancy of roughly thirty years, rising infertility, and hope in Kim's
   molecular research. The research archive then shows the same work condemned
   as heresy, privately sponsored through the head of state's aide, placed
   under the Human Evolution Office, and seized by special forces in 6083.
7. **The safeguards are gathered into one hand.** Directive 0320 turns failed
   contact into permission to launch. The four-point authority record shows
   separate safety powers consolidated at the Defense Archive under emergency
   law. The system retains the appearance of distributed consent after one
   office has made every answer its own.
8. **6083 - Readiness becomes release.** Aron admits that his faith in
   deterrence was wrong as contradictory launch reports fill the warning net.
   The automated record confirms strategic release, counterstrikes, loss of
   guidance, and the sealing of the reserve magazines. Lena's unsent message
   apologizes to Mira for what their generation placed into the sky.

Miang's manipulation is therefore shown through process rather than a
villain's confession: access, emergency language, selective sponsorship,
consolidated authority, and carefully directed fear. Some employees resist,
some rationalize their part, and others find hope in work that has not yet
been corrupted. They remain people living inside the catastrophe rather than
mouthpieces who know the whole Xenogears cosmology.

## Active dungeon continuity

The complete live path now carries that history from entrance to ending:

1. At the access tunnel, Elly immediately identifies the Mass Driver and the
   present objective: Taura's capsule must reach the upper atmosphere so its
   nanomachines can spread throughout the world. The opening does not mislabel
   the facility as a railway or freight depot.
2. The lower works and eight archive annexes reveal the story in chronological
   fragments while the player restores auxiliary and accelerator power.
   Directive 0320 and all four authority points are learned through records
   whose political meaning is part of the same mail narrative.
3. In the Defense Archive, Citan is already waiting beside the console. His
   conversation begins only when the player approaches and talks to him. He
   remains a solid actor until the complete directive and four separate
   authority entries are accepted, then rejoins the moving party.
4. The valid code cannot be completed after only the first letter. The
   Guardian appears because its emergency law has no ending date, not because
   the party entered the wrong answer. Defeating it opens the paired west
   bulkhead and the route through five reserve magazines.
5. Each magazine presents its narration automatically on a forward entry.
   Returning south skips the previous room's scene, and crossing either
   boundary transitions by proximity without requiring another button press.
   The five scenes move from shock at the surviving rockets, through proof of
   release and counterstrikes, to Elly's graveyard image and her decision to
   give the machine a different purpose.
6. Central Control first preserves a launch director's admission that the
   nuclear release was a choice. Citan then confirms Taura's payload link, the
   upper-atmosphere trajectory, and the intended removal of humanity's
   Limiter.
7. Elly experiences voices around the old console. The scene shows frightened
   operators recognizing the consequences while Miang gently turns every
   objection back into an argument for immediate release. The director finally
   orders Directive 0320 executed.
8. Emeralda asks whether the vision was real. Elly does not know, and Citan
   explicitly refuses to call it a record. He distinguishes the uncertain
   afterimage from the surviving archives, which do support the conclusion
   that the ruler's aide guided each step toward war.
9. Elly answers the past with the dungeon's central reversal: the same machine
   once used to destroy life will now be used to heal, returning people the
   ability to choose their own future. The launch then hands control to the
   released game's Mass Driver event.

## Character and narrative controls

The active script keeps each voice within a distinct dramatic role:

- Elly begins with what the human evidence means, speaks in restrained images,
  and does not pretend certainty about the afterimage.
- Citan separates evidence from inference, supplies technical context, and
  avoids claiming knowledge the archives cannot support.
- Emeralda speaks briefly and concretely, especially when the records touch
  Kim, children, or choices adults have hidden behind systems.
- Miang does not announce a secret plan. She asks leading questions, turns
  genuine suffering into political pressure, and lets the officers believe
  the final order is their own.
- The employees disagree in recognizable ways: resistance, fear,
  rationalization, professional duty, regret, and fragile hope.

This structure also avoids overstating the present mission as a permanent cure
for every consequence of the Limiter. Citan states the specific action:
Taura's nanomachines remove the Limiter seal. Elly's language of healing is the
moral contrast requested for the scene, while her final emphasis remains the
restoration of human choice.

## Retail launch handoff

The v62 ending retains the already validated retail handoff. After Elly
commits the capsule, the standalone completion flag and retail Mass Driver
return flag are set and the game invokes the released world-map Mass Driver
event at position `0x0FFF`, mode `17`.

That event shows the canonical launch and atmospheric nanomachine dispersal.
Its guarded return through field 617 preserves Elly, Emeralda, and Citan and
restores playable control without replacing the retail animation with a
newly-authored imitation.
